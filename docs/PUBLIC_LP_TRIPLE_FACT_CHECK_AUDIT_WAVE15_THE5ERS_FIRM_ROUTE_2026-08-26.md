### Check 2 — current The5ers Futures consistency FAQ
`https://www.the5ers.com/futures-faqs/what-is-the-consistency-rule/`

Futures uses a **40%** consistency rule based on a single profitable trade relative to total profitable trades/profits.

This is not the same as Summer 50%.

### Check 3 — current High Stakes / Growth / Bootcamp official rules
Current High Stakes, Growth and Bootcamp product/rule surfaces do not define a universal company-wide 50% consistency rule or a universal single payout cap matching Summer.

`1日50% consistency・Payout capに注意` is overgeneralized as company-level wording.

Status：`CORRECTION_REQUIRED / SCOPE_ERROR`

- remove the company-wide numeric `50%`

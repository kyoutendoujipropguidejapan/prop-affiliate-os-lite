## 1. Minimum trading days article

Public article correctly states:
- minimum trading days may prevent completion even after target is hit
- a trading day and a profitable day are not necessarily the same concept
- `no minimum trading days` does not imply easy payout
- definitions vary by Firm/Plan

### Check 1 — Fintokei English Help
Current official minimum-days FAQ defines a trading day and uses 3 days for current post-2026-07-15 accounts, with cohort differences preserved.

Official:
`https://support.fintokei.com/en/articles/8428030-what-does-the-rule-minimum-trading-days-mean-3-or-5-days`

### Check 2 — FTM English FAQ
Current official FAQ explicitly separates:
- Nitro evaluation: no minimum days
- Nitro simulated funded payout: 5 qualifying days, each >=0.5%
- 2-Step Plus evaluation: 3 days/phase
- funded payout: 5 qualifying days
- Instant plans: 5 qualifying days for payout

Official:
`https://fundedtradermarkets.com/faq/is-there-a-requirement-for-a-minimum-number-of-trading-days`

### Check 3 — SuperFunded Rules V2.0
Current Rules defines minimum trading days differently by program/stage:
- 1 Step evaluation 3
- 2 Step evaluation 4
- funded 1 Step 3
- funded 2 Step 5

Official:
`https://superfunded.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SuperFunded-Rules-and-Conditions.pdf`

### Result

The article's generic framing is appropriate because it does not universalize one number or one counting method.

Status:

## 2. News trading article

Public article says common handling can include:
- allowed
- time-window restrictions
- holding allowed while opening/closing is restricted
- prohibited/restricted depending on program/stage

It repeatedly instructs readers to check Plan/Stage/Order scope rather than treating `Allowed` as universal.

### Check 1 — Fintokei English Help
Current general rule allows opening/closing before, during and after significant news events, while separate risk/consistency restrictions may apply individually.

Official:
`https://support.fintokei.com/en/articles/6538843-can-trade-any-time-hold-positions-over-weekends-and-trade-during-news`

### Check 2 — SuperFunded English FAQ / Rules
Current official FAQ says news trading is allowed in challenge stage but restricted in funded stage around high-impact news. Rules V2.0 defines a 10-minute before/after restriction.

Official:
`https://superfunded.com/faqs/`
`https://superfunded.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SuperFunded-Rules-and-Conditions.pdf`

### Check 3 — FTM English FAQ
Current official FAQ states news trading is allowed across programs, with volatility/slippage caution.

Official:
`https://fundedtradermarkets.com/faq`

### Result

The article's conditional framework is supported by materially different current official implementations across Firms.
